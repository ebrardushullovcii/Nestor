## What loses matches

- Genesys's labelled US-remote lead full-stack job fails reading with “This
  listing needs a browser read”, despite full JSON-LD being recorded. Its five
  sampled jobs are all unread on the HTTP lane. Runware's relevant staff role
  screens/fits, but is absent from collected inventory. These are the provider
  paths being repaired in PR #137; this baseline does not borrow its code.
- Close ranking admits Automattic's PHP/generalist software job (72), Ashby's
  visual-design engineer (70), and Grafana's Go/plugin role (58) above some
  labelled React product matches. The explicit narrow request is underweighted.
  Ashby's staff product match scores 62 for scope stretch; PostHog product work
  scores 58 for backend/agent gaps. Grafana Cloud Observability scores 48 for Go
  and its EST-only line, despite comparable-language wording and a one-hour
  Chicago timezone difference. The next search-quality change must preserve
  genuine country restrictions while using judgment about overlap hours.
- These are agent-authored relevance labels, not independent human consensus.
  PostHog's agent/API experience and on-call requirements, Grafana's timezone
  line, and staff scope are deliberate judgment boundaries. Their full text,
  labels, model explanations and scores are retained for review. Do not silently
  move a label to make the ranking pass.

## Reproduce

`pnpm search:eval` runs production code against the committed recordings and
exact model-response tape, with network disabled. It reports historical tokens,
zero fresh calls, current replay wall time and the original active baseline
wall time. It exits **1** because the close-match top-ten target is missed.
That is a recorded product result, not a failed harness installation.

The JSON report includes every sampled disposition and fit. Changed prompts
fail exact replay and require a new explicitly budgeted recording. Offline
replay always uses the recorded model route; it does not validate later changes
to provider routing or claim their savings. Evaluate those with a new recording.
