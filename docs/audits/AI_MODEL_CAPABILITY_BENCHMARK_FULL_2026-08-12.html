Benchmark result and final product decision

Benchmark winner: Sol Low.

Sol Low delivered the strongest combined operational result: the most direct successes, the fewest fallbacks and guarded rejections, the best aggregate objective scores, and the lowest median latency. This does not mean it is universally smarter; it means it worked with the current product contracts more reliably and efficiently.

Final product decision: Luna High for all compatible text and vision work.

After reviewing the completed benchmark, the product owner selected Luna High because its price is materially preferable and its quality is sufficient. This accepted cost-and-product choice supersedes the benchmark's default-routing recommendation without rewriting the empirical result. Audio transcription remains local Whisper or another audio-capable model.

Do not make Luna Max the default.

Luna Max took 2.34× as long at the median as Sol Low, reached a 124.72-second p95, and produced no overall quality or reliability gain. Its only reviewed win was a narrow specialist advantage on Interview screenshot vision.

What actually failed

The largest problems are integration problems

Typed output and attribution

  • Résumé-vision candidates do not reliably satisfy the product contract.
  • Résumé generation returned partial contributions and fell back in all 30 cases.
  • Guided Edits and Profile Copilot often produced unsupported mutation shapes.
  • All browser visual outputs failed the safe observation schema.
  • One Luna Max vision fallback was incorrectly labeled as a direct success.

Browser and evidence handling

  • Discovery usually exhausted its six-step budget or failed extraction.
  • Every lane missed a visible relative apply URL.
  • Luna source-debug final-review calls repeatedly aborted while Sol completed.
  • Interview cues need tighter grounding against outcome inflation.
  • Screenshot vision missed part of the cropped-error accessibility signal.

These failures should not be “fixed” by selecting a more expensive model. The product boundary must first accept, validate, and attribute outputs correctly.

Reliability detail

Why Luna Max lost despite deeper reasoning

Luna Max produced twelve raw HTTP failures: nine aborted source-debug final-review calls, one 180-second résumé-vision timeout, one 35-second job-extraction timeout, and one 35-second agentic-discovery timeout. Résumé text import alone had a 111.2-second median, compared with 34.1 seconds for High and 26.5 seconds for Sol.

Method and limits

What this proves—and what it does not

Strong evidence

  • Exactly 330 unique outcomes out of 330 expected.
  • Ten behavior-backed synthetic cases per capability and model lane.
  • Raw provider contribution separated from guarded product output and fallback.
  • Seed-balanced schedules, serial execution within each lane, and resumable artifacts.
  • Independent qualitative review across all lanes and every capability.
  • No personal profile, résumé, credentials, authenticated state, or application actions.

Important limits

  • One full synthetic run per lane; no repeated-trial confidence intervals yet.
  • Model family and reasoning effort are paired, so they are not independently causal.
  • Typed-contract and browser orchestration failures confound several capabilities.
  • Objective scores are triage signals, not a replacement for raw-output review.
  • Synthetic evidence does not establish live-site or private-résumé generalization.

Preserved follow-up

What should be improved next

  • Repair typed provider contracts for résumé vision/generation, Guided Edits, Profile Copilot, and browser visual analysis.
  • Fix discovery extraction and tool-budget failures, then repeat only the affected discovery cases.
  • Add relative apply-URL resolution and a focused extraction regression.
  • Make fallback attribution exact so deterministic recovery can never count as direct model success.
  • Strengthen source-debug abort diagnostics and Interview cue/screenshot grounding.
  • Add runner, prompt, fixture, and grader hashes to manifests.
  • Repeat seeds for variance only after integration defects are fixed.
