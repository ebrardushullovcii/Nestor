# ADR 0041: AI judgment with mechanical collection of published job data

Status: accepted with amendments (2026-10-02 through 2026-10-25); ADR 0051 removes unchecked-line approval gates. Partly superseded for Aggressive resumes: local number checks replace model claim checks (588a399e), and code adds listing skills and cleans repeated or generic prose (fef8af09). Other levels retain model claim checks; the discovery decision below remains accepted.

Discovery amended by the owner's rewrite request (2026-10-25): deterministic collection is the default; AI owns profession screening, uncertain interpretation and fit. This supersedes the earlier ban on mechanical card collection and the earlier repeated fit passes.

## Context

The second usability round found jobs saved with a logo's initials as the company ("CR" for Cresta), with a board's tag chips as the location, and with a "DE" country code read as Delaware. Each came from code that parsed page text by rules: the `scan_cards` tool saved repeated DOM cards without a model call, and its parsed company and location were also handed to the extractor as evidence. The first fixes added more rules on top (initials patterns, tag-chip class names, class-name reads on detail pages). The same pattern sits behind other findings: a keyword list read "privacy notice" as a notice period, and title words decided role matches.

The agent already sees the whole page. It can tell a logo from a company, a tag from a place, and Berlin's DE from Delaware's.

## Decision

- **Collect before judging.** Read public provider feeds, JSON-LD and ordinary HTTP list pages without AI. Follow observed pagination, never board-specific guessed search routes. Semantic HTML can supply title links and visible card facts. Corroborate the hiring employer against the full listing; a board or ATS brand is not the employer. Use browser navigation only when collection cannot finish a site.
- **Published data stays intact.** Provider APIs and JSON-LD are evidence, including original identifiers and application links. Capture the entire visible listing as well: a sparse JSON-LD description can omit requirements. Store that full text in job_details.description for fit and resume writing.
- **Spend AI on uncertainty.** A complete semantic listing body needs no model transcription. Read ambiguous metadata in batches when published facts and corroborated HTML are insufficient. Preserve the body mechanically instead of asking the model to rewrite it. Failed reads stay retryable and never turn card text into a supposedly complete description.
- **Screen professions together.** Batch new titles against target and related roles, sharing the profession decision across common rank variants. Reject only clearly unrelated work. A different level, place, employment type or expected score is for fit, not a reason to lose a related listing. Persist unrelated cards beside jobs, keyed by URL, material card fingerprint and role-goals key. Changed cards or role goals can be reconsidered. Saved listings, including hidden jobs, are skipped before detail reads.
- **Fit once after merging.** Read plausible listings in full, save and merge their identities, then judge only merged jobs that have no stored fit. Batch full listing bodies with the person's profile and goals. Store the score, concise explanation, role, preferences and evidence coverage; preserve low scores. A repeat search, shortlist or resume write reuses this judgment. No formula recomputes or caps it. New related jobs hidden by a retention choice still receive a fit score.
- **The model answers application questions.** The apply agent gets all of the person's facts (contact details, eligibility, saved answers, roles, education, the resume going out) and answers the form from them, a page at a time with `fill_fields`. What it types or chooses goes in when it is one of the person's stored values word for word (an email, a phone number, a link, a saved sentence), or the person's saved answer to that exact question, or the fact check (a model reading the answers against the person's facts, one call per batch) supports it. An unsupported answer is not entered and the model is told why. Without a fact check only stored values go in. No per-field rule picks an answer: the answer sourcing, work-country, phone-country and work-history rules are removed. A required field still empty after one reminder goes to the person with the form.
- **The model says which questions the person's permissions cover.** The model classifies each new question meaning, batching unread questions on the page: whether it asks about the person's pay, and whether it is a declaration and which kind. The pay switch and declaration approvals act on that reading, in any wording or language. Meaning may be reused across steps and applications when the question, its options, ancestry and relevant instructions are unchanged. Rebind source descriptors and the hiring country to current facts on each use; cached meaning carries no answers or authority. Classification sees source descriptors rather than resume, posting or scalar fact values; writing and grounding retain the full evidence. If the model cannot be asked, the keyword lists stand in, so a permission is never left unguarded.
- **The person decides what to keep.** Pickiness controls the screen's fit floor; it does not silently discard related listings. A posting-age choice can save a job hidden with a visible, reversible reason. Per-source notes report coverage, new related jobs, unrelated cards, already-seen listings and failed reads.
- **Code keeps safety and mechanics.** Final submit authority, consent, credentials, allowed origins, identity of the same URL, counts, dates and time zones, and file fingerprints stay deterministic. So do the person's own switches: pay they keep to themselves, declarations they did not approve, companies they blocked and places they excluded.
- **The model reads the resume.** Each import section (identity and contact, work history, background) is read by the model with the whole document available. A call that fails for a passing reason is asked again; a section still unread is named to the person and nothing is guessed for it. On the model path there is no regex pass, no rule-read candidate beside the model's, no rule fill-in of company and place, no regex split of title from employer, and no rule check of the model's values against the resume text; reconciliation keeps only structure (merging, de-duplication, the person's own edits and identity). Without any model (tests, or a build with no AI) the rule reader stands in, as before.
- **Bound model work.** Full bodies are batched without truncating their requirements; both batch size and simultaneous requests are bounded. The browser fallback retains bounded page reads and conversation compaction. Repeats of unchanged replica inventories need neither a detail read nor a model call.
- **Browser fallback reads long pages in parts.** Preserve links beside visible text and use semantic article/list-item boundaries. Reuse identical text and links, advance past unchanged pages, and report partial coverage honestly. The fallback collects cards without judging fit; the same profession screen and full-read steps used by HTTP collection follow it. Source-debug learning remains available to navigation.
- **A failed model call is a failure, not a rule guess.** When the model cannot be asked, the person is told and what they had stays: page reads report the failure instead of "no jobs", import conflicts stay in setup review, the profile and resume assistants say nothing was changed, a resume the AI could not write leaves the job's resume as it was (a first attempt shows a retryable failure), and fit stays unjudged. No built-in parser, editor, extractor or resume writer answers in the model's place.
- **The model checks resume claims.** A tailored resume keeps the model's lines as written. Before a draft is validated and kept, the model checks each generated line against the person's evidence (profile, saved records, imported resume), many lines per call: supported, a stretch the person confirms, or unsupported. The verdicts are stored with the draft by the line's content hash and reused until the wording, the evidence or the tailoring strength changes; the Resume Assistant's proposals are checked the same way before they are offered. The writing agent does not repair what the check flags (ADR 0049, amended 2026-10-21). When a generated resume is kept, any line the check still did not pass (and, outside aggressive tailoring, any stretch) gets the checker's own fix: rewritten to what the evidence backs, or hidden when nothing in it can be kept; the rewrites are checked once more, and a rewrite that still fails returns to the saved wording or is left off (ADR 0051). A line the person wrote, a line that repeats their records or resume word for word, and a skill on their profile need no check. A generated line not checked yet waits for the person. No token-overlap classifier, listing-skill injection, job-text bleed detector or metric rule judges or removes lines any more.
- **The model's routes decide where a search or source check starts.** The kind the source check's review gave each route (listing, search, collection) stands over what its address looks like, and a source check's phases start from those recorded routes. No keyword is picked by rule and typed into a search address: the search agent uses the site's own search with the person's goals in front of it.
- **The model notes unfinished resume writing.** The fact check also says when a generated line is a keyword list, a fragment, filler or first-person prose, and gives a fix that a generated resume gets. No word rule drops a line or swaps a summary out, and the person's own lines get no note.
- **The model organizes what a source check learned.** The check's final review keeps, files and reconciles the check's notes and says whether a future search can rely on them; its lists and that call stand. No keyword list filters learned lines, scores their quality or decides when they are validated. Without the review the notes stay a draft and say so.

## Consequences

Ordinary result pages cost HTTP reads rather than model navigation and transcription. New profession decisions and merged full-text jobs are batched. Fit scores appear as batches finish. Existing fit stays stable; an unscored job remains visibly unscored if the model fails. A form page still uses model classification for its questions.

There are no old-data migrations or search-plan allowances. Indexed lookups recognize saved URLs and rejected cards. Searches write only new sightings, new jobs, their fit updates and their own run counts; they do not reload or synchronize the whole workspace.

A form page is usually filled in one or two model turns plus one fact-check call, instead of a turn and a lookup per field. Required questions the model could not answer from the facts reach the person once, after a single reminder, rather than the run stalling on them.

What stays deterministic on purpose: the person's own lists (blocked companies matched by name, excluded places matched by their own words), the arithmetic of pay against a saved minimum, the cross-listing check that drops a pay band every listing of a site shares, the keyword fallback for pay and declaration questions when the model cannot be asked, and the label-to-option spelling match when a dropdown words the model's answer differently. Still rule-based: the base draft built from the profile when there is no model at all or the listing text was never captured.
